METEOROLOGICAL FEATURES Yesterday's trough of westerly wave over the northern parts of the country still persists. II. METEOROLOGICAL OUTLOOK Isolated rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over the upper catchments of river Indus, Jhelum and Chenab, Islamabad, Kashmir, Gilgit Baltistan, Upper Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and north Punjab. III. HYDROLOGICAL SITUATION at PST All major rivers are flowing in their normal flows at all gauging stations. IV. HYDROLOGICAL OUTLOOK Flows in all major rivers are expected to remain within the normal flow range at their respective rim stations. V. WEATHER FORECAST FOR -HOUR Isolated rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over the upper catchments of river Indus, Jhelum and Chenab, Islamabad, Kashmir, Gilgit Baltistan, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Abbottabad, Batagram, Chitral, Haripur, Kohistan Lower, Kohistan, Mansehra, Shangla, Swat and Dir. WEATHER OUTLOOK FOR NEXT -HOURS Mainly dry weather is expected over the most parts of the country, however, Isolated rain-wind/thunderstorm is expected over the upper catchments of river Indus and Jhelum, Kashmir, Gilgit Baltistan, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Batagram, Chitral, Kohistan, Mansehra, Shangla, Swat and Dir.
